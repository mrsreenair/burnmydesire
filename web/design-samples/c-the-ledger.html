01 / Method

Two punches,
forty seconds.

One rational, one emotional. Neither of them asks you to be stronger than you were five minutes ago.
01

Name it

A photo, a link, or a sentence. A €429 pair of headphones, or €40 of drinks every Friday — recurring cravings are allowed, and they cost the most.

02

Price it properly

The app answers with the compound number: what the money becomes if it stays invested until you're 65. That figure is the shock.

03

Destroy it

Hold your thumb down. A real burning front eats across the image, curls the edge, and leaves ash. The craving has somewhere to end.

02 / The burn

The one screen where
we turn out the lights.

Everything else in the app is daylight and paper. The ritual isn't: the room goes dark and the only light is the fire itself, rendered on the GPU at 60 frames a second — a burning front with a glowing edge, curling paper and embers that drift.

You don't get told the urge will pass. You watch the thing that caused it stop existing.

04 / Privacy

The things you're ashamed of
never leave the phone.

This is a health-grade app about money, drink and craving. It is built so that there is nothing to leak.
No accounts

Nothing to sign into

No email, no password, no sync. There is no backend, so an account would have nothing to unlock.

Encrypted

Sealed on the device

The database is encrypted with a key held in the iOS Keychain. Photos and written thoughts use complete data protection — unreadable while the phone is locked.

Erasable

Burn the whole thing

One confirmed tap destroys the database, the images, the preferences and the key. Nothing survives, because nothing was copied.
